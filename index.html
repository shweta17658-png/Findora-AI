ABOUT THE PROJECT

About ReClaim

ReClaim is a school Lost and Found website developed to make reporting and recovering belongings easier.

The system uses keyword-based matching to identify possible connections between lost and found reports. Students can browse reports and submit return requests, while authorised teachers manage the process.

The main aim is to reduce confusion, save time and encourage students to take responsibility for their belongings.
